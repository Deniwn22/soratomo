Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 25, 2026 · Version 2.1

Short version: SoraTomo uses your GPS location to find nearby aircraft and your camera for AR features. If you join the leaderboard, your chosen callsign and daily score are stored in a cloud database. Most other data stays on your device. No account or real name is ever required.

1. Who We Are

SoraTomo ("the app," "we," "us") is an aircraft spotting application for personal, recreational, and educational use, developed as an independent project. Questions: privacy@soratomo.app

3. Location

SoraTomo requests your device's GPS location to:

  • Request nearby aircraft from ADS-B data providers (your lat/lon is included in each request)
  • Calculate aircraft distances, bearings, and AR overlay positions
  • Snap your leaderboard entry to a geographic region (nearest airport)
  • Record where you were when an aircraft was logged (stored locally only)

Your coordinates are sent to our Netlify proxy server on every data poll (approximately every 2 seconds while the app is open). The proxy forwards a rounded version of your coordinates to adsb.lol and airplanes.live. We do not store your location history. Each request is independent and no movement trail is built.

4. Camera

The app requests camera access to render the augmented reality view. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device. No video, frames, or images are transmitted to any server. When you tap the shutter button, a screenshot is saved to your device's local storage (IndexedDB) only. If you tap Share, you choose where to send it.

5. Leaderboard (Optional)

The leaderboard is opt-in. If you do not set a callsign, no data is sent to our cloud database. If you participate:

  • Your callsign, daily score, date, region label, and random device ID are written to Firebase Firestore (Google)
  • This data is publicly readable to all app users in your region
  • Callsigns must be 2–12 alphanumeric characters and are subject to our content policy
  • You can change your callsign at any time; previous scores under the old callsign are not retroactively updated
  • To request deletion of your leaderboard entries, email privacy@soratomo.app with your device ID (found in browser storage under soratomo_device_id)

6. Third-Party Services

  • adsb.lol — live ADS-B aircraft data. Receives your approximate coordinates per request. adsb.lol
  • airplanes.live — secondary ADS-B source. Same coordinate sharing as above. airplanes.live
  • adsbdb.com — aircraft registration lookup by ICAO hex. Receives aircraft hex codes (not your location). adsbdb.com
  • Firebase / Google — leaderboard database (Firestore). Only used if you join the leaderboard. Firebase Privacy
  • Netlify — hosts the app and proxy functions. Netlify may log request metadata per their policy. Netlify Privacy
  • Google Fonts — loads Orbitron and Exo 2 typefaces from Google's servers. Google Privacy
  • CARTO — map tiles in the logbook map view. CARTO Privacy
  • unpkg (Cloudflare CDN) — serves the Leaflet mapping library used by the logbook map. Receives your IP address when the library loads. Cloudflare Privacy
  • Wikipedia / DuckDuckGo — aircraft type info lookup when you tap "get aircraft info." Wikipedia Privacy

7. Data Retention

  • On-device data — retained until you clear your browser/app storage or use the in-app Clear All function
  • Leaderboard data — each entry is tagged with an expiry date 90 days after your most recent submission and is automatically deleted on or shortly after that date. Submitting a new score refreshes the 90-day window. You may also request earlier deletion at any time (see below), and we may remove entries earlier for policy violations.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The leaderboard requires entry of a callsign — if a child uses the app, a parent or guardian should supervise this feature.

9. Your Rights

For on-device data: use the in-app Clear All button or clear your browser storage. For leaderboard data: email privacy@soratomo.app to request deletion. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above. Significant changes that affect data collection will trigger a re-acknowledgement prompt in the app.
