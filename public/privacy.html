Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026 · Version 1.0

Short version: SoraTomo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party controlled by the developer. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who We Are

SoraTomo ("the app," "we," "us") is an aircraft spotting application for personal, recreational, and educational use. It is developed and operated as an independent project.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your precise or approximate GPS location
  • Your camera feed or any photos taken in the app
  • Your logbook, aircraft sightings, or usage history
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or analytics

There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend database associated with this app.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally in your browser or device storage and never leaves your device:

  • Logbook — aircraft you have spotted, including callsigns, positions, altitudes, speeds, and the location where you spotted them. Stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • Calibration settings — AR compass offset, pitch bias, and camera FOV values. Stored in localStorage.
  • Photo gallery — screenshots taken in camera mode. Stored in localStorage.
  • Disclaimer acknowledgement — a flag indicating you have accepted the disclaimer. Stored in localStorage.

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing your browser/app data for this site.

4. Location (GPS)

The app requests access to your device's GPS location to:

  • Show nearby aircraft relative to your position
  • Orient the AR overlay to your heading and position
  • Record where you were when an aircraft was logged

Your location is processed entirely on-device. It is used solely to compute distances, bearings, and AR projections locally. Location data is not transmitted to any server operated by the developer.

5. Camera

The app requests camera access for the AR camera overlay feature. The camera feed is processed in real time on your device only to render the augmented reality view. No video, frames, or images are transmitted externally by the developer.

When you tap the shutter button, a screenshot is saved to your device's local storage only.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services. Please review their privacy policies independently:

  • adsb.lol — provides live ADS-B aircraft position data. The app sends your approximate GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, and a search radius) to this service to retrieve nearby aircraft. No personally identifying information is sent. See adsb.lol for their terms.
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API — used during AR calibration to find nearby landmarks. Your approximate coordinates are sent to query for landmarks. See OSM Privacy Policy.
  • CartoDB / CARTO — provides map tile imagery in the logbook map view. Tile requests include the map coordinates being viewed, not your personal location. See CARTO Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — loads the Orbitron and Exo 2 typefaces. This involves a request to Google's servers. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Leaflet / unpkg CDN — the mapping library is loaded from unpkg.com. See npm/unpkg Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because no data is collected by the developer, COPPA compliance is inherent to the app's design.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. The app's disclaimer version key will be incremented to prompt re-acknowledgement for significant changes.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Open an issue at the project repository or contact the developer directly through the App Store listing page.
